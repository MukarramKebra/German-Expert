Questions we get every day

How do I make sure a part fits my car?

Pick your car in the finder at the top, search by part number, or send us your VIN (chassis number) on WhatsApp. We confirm the exact part before you pay.

How do I order?

Find the part and tap Enquire. WhatsApp opens with the part details ready to send. We confirm availability, price and delivery, and you pay on delivery or by bank transfer.

Where in Kuwait do you deliver?

Across Kuwait. Message us on WhatsApp with your area and we'll confirm timing, or pick up from our shop in Shuwaikh Industrial Area.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, card on delivery and bank transfer. Payment details are confirmed with you directly on WhatsApp.

Can I return or exchange a part?

All returns need prior approval. Contact us by phone or WhatsApp before sending anything back and we'll arrange it with you.

Do you have trade prices for garages?

Yes. Garages, workshops and fleet operators get trade pricing. Message us to set it up.
