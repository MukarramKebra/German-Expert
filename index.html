Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Where in the UAE do you deliver?

We deliver same day within Sharjah, and within 1-2 business days to Dubai, Ajman, Abu Dhabi, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah. You can also pick up from our Sharjah store.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, card on delivery and bank transfer. Payment details are confirmed with you directly on WhatsApp after you place an order.

How do I make sure a part fits my car?

Filter by your car make, search by part number, or send us your VIN (chassis number) on WhatsApp — we will confirm the exact part before you pay.

Can I return or exchange a part?

Yes. Unused, uninstalled parts in their original packaging can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery. Electrical parts can only be exchanged if faulty. Contact us on WhatsApp to arrange it.

How do I order or get help?

Find the part you need and tap Enquire — it opens WhatsApp with the part details ready to send. Our team confirms availability, price and delivery, and you pay on delivery or by bank transfer.
